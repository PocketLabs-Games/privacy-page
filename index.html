PocketLabs Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PocketLabs ("we", "us", "our") handles information in our mobile games, currently:

  • Slime Sort Color!
  • CapiDoku!

Together, these are referred to as the "Apps". This policy applies to all of them, and to any future PocketLabs apps that link to it.

1. Information We Collect

PocketLabs does not directly collect any personal data from users. No account is required, and you do not need to provide your name, email address or any other personal information to play. We do not sell personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The Apps may save game data locally on your device, such as progress, completed levels, scores or settings (sound, vibration). This data stays on your device only, is not sent to our servers, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

The Apps display ads through third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob). These providers may automatically collect and use certain data, such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to serve ads and measure their performance. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, for example Google's: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads using the advertising ID settings on your Android device (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. In-App Purchases

The Apps offer optional in-app purchases (for example, to remove ads or obtain extras). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. PocketLabs does not receive or store your payment details, such as card numbers.

5. Permissions

The Apps do not request permissions that give access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or personal files. They may use internet access to display ads and process purchases.

6. Children's Privacy

The Apps are not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Because we do not directly collect personal data, we do not store user information on our servers. Data handled by advertising providers and Google Play is subject to those providers' own security measures.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: pocketlabsgames@gmail.com
